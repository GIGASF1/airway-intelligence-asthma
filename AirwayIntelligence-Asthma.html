AIRWAY INTELLIGENCE — ASTHMA

Simulation-based decision training in severe asthma for pulmonary & critical care fellows. Advance through each case by committing evidence-based decisions. Correct choices advance the case and reveal physiology; unsafe choices trigger teaching feedback and, where clinically true, patient deterioration. Every option carries an Evidence popover. Case details stay concealed until you are in the room — commit decisions to find out what happens. Each debrief reports your first-pass accuracy.
